Import with AI

Connect an AI assistant to bring in your existing maintenance records — a spreadsheet, a typed list, or another tracker's export.

  1. In your AI assistant, add a custom connector (also called a remote MCP server).
  2. Paste the address above.
  3. Sign in to Aviary when asked, then click Allow.

Then paste your maintenance spreadsheet or list and ask it to import into Aviary. You'll see a preview first — nothing is added until you say so.
